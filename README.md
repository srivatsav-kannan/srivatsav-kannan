# Srivatsav Kannan

I build research prototypes at the intersection of medical AI, computer vision,
clinical decision support, and model fairness. My public repositories emphasize
reproducible setup, honest evaluation, and clear boundaries between research,
applications, APIs, and historical work.

## Featured projects

### Biomedical waste segregation

Computer-vision research for classifying biomedical waste, with a separate
public-facing project site.

- [Research and model evaluation](https://github.com/srivatsav-kannan/biomedical-waste-segregation-research)
- [Project website](https://github.com/srivatsav-kannan/biomedical-waste-segregation-website)

### Medical AI fairness audit

A research and tooling project for auditing subgroup performance and exploring
debiasing workflows in medical imaging.

- [Audit and debiasing research](https://github.com/srivatsav-kannan/medical-ai-fairness-audit-debiasing)
- [Interactive audit dashboard](https://github.com/srivatsav-kannan/medical-ai-fairness-audit-dashboard)

### ZEBRA rare-disease triage

An interpretable rare-disease decision-support prototype that maps clinical
phenotypes to HPO, ranks a differential deterministically, and chooses
cost-aware next questions. This is my maintained fork of the original
NightHacks project and is not a medical device.

- [Code, documentation, and verification](https://github.com/srivatsav-kannan/zebra-rare-disease-triage)

## Repository map

Repository names follow a role-based convention:

- `*-research` — canonical research, experiments, and evaluation
- `*-api`, `*-mobile`, `*-website`, `*-dashboard` — deployable components
- `*-archive-*` — preserved historical work, not the canonical implementation

Additional clinical research projects remain private while data rights,
publication readiness, and security are reviewed. Public does not mean
clinically validated; each repository documents its own evidence and limits.
